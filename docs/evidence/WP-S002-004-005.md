# WP-S002-004-005 — Terrain / hydrology / traversal evidence

Classification: **MIXED**.

This package replaces the single prototype sine-wave river with a bounded, deterministic catchment authority. `src/surface.ts` owns natural elevation, seeded drainage recipes, tributaries/local lakes, water classification, slope/cliff classification, freshwater distance and base traversal legality. `src/world.ts` consumes that shared surface for terrain height, biome meaning and cell walkability so rendering and traversal do not maintain competing terrain truths.

## Required functional evidence

- Campaign SEED + foundation version + canonical coordinates are the only foundation inputs; no random-number API, camera, renderer, timing or load order input is used.
- Drainage recipes have deterministic IDs, strictly descending main river beds and actual macro-water outlets.
- Multiple seeded drainage systems occur across all three continents, with tributaries and local freshwater lakes where selected by the SEED.
- Water is non-walkable; steep canonical cliff samples are non-walkable; difficult slopes remain distinguishable from blocked cliffs.
- Longitude wrap produces identical height, water, traversal and catchment identity.
- Freshwater lookup is region bounded and the drainage cache is capped.
- A strict majority of generated villages fall within the configured authoritative freshwater-access radius.

## Required visual evidence

Fresh screenshots must be captured and actually inspected for both WebGPU and WebGL2 on desktop and phone. The deterministic gallery targets:

1. Realm-scale coastlines.
2. Province-scale river/tributary context.
3. Village-scale riverbank.
4. Province-scale seeded freshwater lake.
5. Province-scale mountain system.
6. Street-scale cliff / steep terrain.
7. Village-scale settlement near authoritative freshwater.
8. River outlet reaching macro water.

Visual acceptance is at least **8/10**, with no obvious grid/lattice pattern, stair-step coast or river artifacts, cracks, water discontinuities, unreadable cliff relief, clipping or major responsive UI obstruction.

Physical phone/tablet FPS, thermal behavior and device memory remain **UNVERIFIED** unless measured on real hardware; CI software rendering proves correctness and regression behavior only.
