# WP-S002-004-005 — Terrain / hydrology / traversal evidence

Classification: **MIXED**.

This package replaces the single prototype sine-wave river with a bounded, deterministic catchment authority. `src/hydrology.ts` owns natural elevation, seeded drainage recipes, tributaries/local lakes, water classification, slope/cliff classification and freshwater distance without importing settlement geography. `src/surface.ts` composes the later settlement/road earthworks over that natural authority and exposes the final height/traversal surface. `src/geography.ts` consults the pure hydrology authority for settlement legality, while `src/world.ts`, terrain refinement and logical cells consume the composed surface so rendering and traversal do not maintain competing terrain truths.

## Required functional evidence

- Campaign SEED + foundation version + canonical coordinates are the only foundation inputs; no random-number API, camera, renderer, timing or load order input is used.
- Drainage recipes have deterministic IDs, strictly descending main river beds and actual macro-water outlets.
- Multiple seeded drainage systems occur across all three continents, with tributaries and local freshwater lakes where selected by the SEED.
- Water is non-walkable; steep canonical cliff samples are non-walkable; difficult slopes remain distinguishable from blocked cliffs.
- Longitude wrap produces identical height, water, traversal and catchment identity.
- Freshwater lookup is region bounded and the drainage cache is capped.
- A strict majority of generated villages fall within the configured authoritative freshwater-access radius.
- Settlement candidates reject canonical water/cliff samples; the old sine-river avoidance rule is not used for authoritative siting.
- `world.heightAt`, `world.biomeAt`, logical cell walkability and refined local terrain consume the same composed surface.
- Current political registries, generalized road distance, bounded vegetation batching and continuous-zoom behavior from `main` are preserved.
