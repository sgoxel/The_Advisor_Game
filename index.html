WP-007C Rounded Terrain Blending
Neighbor terrain is revealed through rounded SVG masks instead of decorative contour loopsWAITING

Terrain boundaries now use real material blending shapes: rounded side edges, rounded 90-degree corners, three-sided peninsula/inlet masks and rounded isolated-island masks. The neighboring terrain keeps its own color and SVG texture inside the blend.

Water/grass, dirt/grass, forest/grass, farmland/grass and similar terrain pairs therefore meet on a curved material boundary rather than a square tile edge.
